## 摘要
量子计算引入了专门的软件栈和编程接口，它们通常与特定生态系统和硬件后端紧密耦合，限制了互操作性和软件复用。对于 HPCQC 应用开发者而言，这使量子加速器集成到现有应用中变得更加复杂。现有量子框架还使用特定生态系统的电路表示和编译流水线，阻碍了与已有异构编译器基础设施的集成。我们提出 OpenMQPI，这是一种基于 MLIR 的量子编程 OpenMP 扩展，利用 OpenMP 标准化的目标卸载基础设施将量子资源集成到 HPC 应用中。OpenMQPI 引入了 QPIR，一种轻量级 MLIR 方言，用于在 OpenMP 目标区域中表示量子电路；还引入了一个编译器流水线，执行量子特定优化并将电路降级为兼容 OpenMP 卸载的可执行文件。在运行时，MQSS 插件使生成的可执行文件能够通过 OpenMP 目标卸载模型将量子电路卸载到量子硬件。我们使用 VQE、QFT 和 Deutsch-Joseph 算法在 5、10、15 和 20 个量子比特下评估 OpenMQPI，并将执行时间与 Qiskit 和 CUDA Quantum 进行比较。OpenMQPI 在所有测试的量子比特配置下实现了最低的平均 VQE 执行时间。在 20 个量子比特时，它需要 5.18 秒，而 Qiskit 为 5.83 秒，CUDA Quantum 为 7.39 秒。对于 QFT 和 Deutsch-Joseph，OpenMQPI 的执行时间与 Qiskit 相当，且低于 CUDA Quantum。

## Abstract
Quantum computing introduces specialized software stacks and programming interfaces that are often tightly coupled to specific ecosystems and hardware backends, limiting interoperability and software reuse. For HPCQC application developers, this complicates the integration of quantum accelerators into existing applications. Existing quantum frameworks also use ecosystem-specific circuit representations and compilation pipelines, hindering integration with established heterogeneous compiler infrastructures. We propose OpenMQPI, an MLIR-based OpenMP extension for quantum programming that leverages OpenMP's standardized target offloading infrastructure to integrate quantum resources into HPC applications. OpenMQPI introduces QPIR, a lightweight MLIR dialect for representing quantum circuits within OpenMP target regions, and a compiler pipeline that performs quantum-specific optimizations and lowers circuits to executables compatible with OpenMP offloading. At runtime, an MQSS plugin enables the generated executable to offload quantum circuits to quantum hardware through the OpenMP target offloading model. We evaluate OpenMQPI using the VQE, QFT, and Deutsch-Jozsa algorithms with 5, 10, 15, and 20 qubits, comparing execution times against Qiskit and CUDA Quantum. OpenMQPI achieves the lowest average VQE execution time across all tested qubit configurations. At 20 qubits, it requires 5.18 s, compared with 5.83 s for Qiskit and 7.39 s for CUDA Quantum. For QFT and Deutsch-Jozsa, OpenMQPI achieves execution times comparable to Qiskit and lower than those of CUDA Quantum.
